# IRC Channel

Join us on **#nginx** on [Libera Chat](https://libera.chat/).

## Code of Conduct

- **Be Considerate**: We're all here to help and learn
- **Be Respectful**: Disagreement is fine; rudeness is not
- **Be Constructive**: Avoid flame wars and personal attacks
- **Be Family Friendly**: No inappropriate topics or language
- **Stay On Topic**: Support questions take priority over chatter
- **Don't Spam**: No excessive nick changes, away messages, or repeated questions
- **Use Common Sense**: Treat others as you want to be treated

We follow [Libera Chat's policies](https://libera.chat/policies) and [channel guidelines](https://libera.chat/guidelines).

## Appeals Process

If you have an issue with a moderator action:

1. **Stay calm** - No caps or swearing
2. **PM the operator** - Mistakes happen; a polite conversation often resolves issues
3. **Join #nginx-master** - If step 2 fails, another operator may help

Read the [IRC Operators](irc-ops.md) document for more information.
